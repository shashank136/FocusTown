# Focus Town support

Focus Town is an offline focus timer: you start a session, builders build, and you leave your phone alone.

## How it works

- Pick 10 to 120 minutes (steps of 5). Longer sessions build bigger buildings.
- Lock your phone and put it face down. Locking is fine for as long as you like.
- Going to the home screen or another app counts as leaving. You get 10 seconds free, then one 60-second pass per session. After that your builders stop and a ruin is left.
- Once a week you can arm an emergency pause before leaving, which allows one absence of up to 10 minutes.
- A ruin can be cleared with a 10-minute repair session.
- Build on any free plot. When you want more room, or space between your buildings, use Extend land. Your streak grows with every day you focus.

## Common questions

**My builders stopped but I only locked my phone.**
Make sure the app was open when you locked the phone. If you pressed Home first and then locked, the app counts that as leaving.

**I don't get warnings.**
Open Settings > Notifications > Focus Town and allow notifications.

**I don't see the lock screen countdown.**
Open Settings > Focus Town and make sure Live Activities are on.

**Can I get my town back after deleting the app?**
No. Everything is stored on the phone only.
