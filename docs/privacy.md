# Focus Town privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Focus Town does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

## What stays on your phone

- Your town, focus sessions, streak and settings are saved in a database on your phone. They never leave it.
- Focus Town has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It makes no network connections.
- A small log file on your phone records app events (for example "session started") to help find bugs. It stays on your phone. You can copy it from the debug screen and send it to the developer if you choose to; nothing is sent automatically.

## What the app uses

- **Notifications:** to warn you before your builders stop when you leave the app during a session. They are scheduled on your phone.
- **Lock screen status (Live Activity):** to show the countdown of a running session on the lock screen. It is drawn from data on your phone.
- **Screen lock and app switching:** the app notices when the screen locks or when you leave the app, so it can tell a break from leaving. Nothing about this is stored beyond your session record.
- **Phone calls:** the app notices that a call started, so a call does not count as leaving. It does not read who called or any call content.

## Children

Focus Town collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app and all its data is removed from your phone.
